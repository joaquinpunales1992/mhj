Method and limits

This is desk research. Nobody has visited the property, and no part of this report is a structural, pest or boundary survey. It is compiled from the source listing as published, cross-referenced against {{ inventory_size }} comparable listings in our own inventory.

Where the listing is silent, the report says so rather than estimating. Statements about what a designation generally means are general, not a determination about this parcel: only the relevant municipal office can give that.
