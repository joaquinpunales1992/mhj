1. What this site is

My Akiya in Japan collects houses for sale in Japan — many of them akiya — from public Japanese property websites, translates and reorganises them, and puts them somewhere an international buyer can search. Using the site means accepting these terms.

2. What we are not

This is the most important section on the page, so it is near the top.

  • We are not the seller of any house listed here, and we are not a licensed Japanese real-estate agent (宅地建物取引業者). We do not take part in the sale itself.
  • The listings are not ours. They come from third-party sites. A house may already be sold, withdrawn, or described inaccurately at the source, and we retire listings when we notice, not the moment it happens.
  • Prices in US dollars are a guide only. The real price is in yen. We convert at a fixed rate for comparison; it is not a quote, and the rate on the day you buy will differ.
  • Nothing here is legal, tax, immigration or financial advice. Buying property in Japan has consequences in all four areas, and you should take professional advice on your own situation.

Verify anything that matters to your decision — the price, the condition, the boundaries, the zoning, whether it is still for sale — before spending money on the strength of it.

4. What you can pay for

Current prices are on the pricing page, and the price shown when you buy is the price that applies.

  • Pro — a recurring subscription, billed by PayPal, that lifts the limit on listing views and adds the subscriber newsletter and other members' features. Cancel any time in PayPal; access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • A consultation — a scheduled call about buying in Japan. It is our experience of the process, not professional advice, and we cannot act for you in a transaction.
  • A desk report — written research on one listing, using public sources and the listing itself. It is not a survey and nobody visits the property.
  • An inspection — arranging for someone to look at a property in person. What is included, and what it costs, is quoted case by case before you pay.

These are independent of each other. None is a prerequisite for any other, and none of them makes us your agent.

5. Cancellations and refunds

Cancel Pro any time from PayPal; it stops the next payment and leaves the current period intact. We do not refund part-months.

A consultation can be moved or cancelled up to 24 hours before it starts, for a refund or another slot. Later than that, or if you do not appear, the slot is used. Desk reports are refundable until we start work; once delivered they are not.

If we get something wrong, tell us. We would rather fix it than argue about a policy.
