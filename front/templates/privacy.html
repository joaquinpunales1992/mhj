Privacy policy

How we handle personal information.

Last updated {{ last_updated }}.

Who we are

My Akiya in Japan (akiyainjapan.com) lists houses for sale in Japan and offers related services. {% if legal_entity %}It is operated by {{ legal_entity }}.{% endif %} For anything to do with this policy, including any request about your own information, contact hello@akiyainjapan.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address, and either a password (stored only in hashed form) or the fact that you signed in with a third-party provider.
  • Information you send us — what you enter into our enquiry, booking and request forms, such as your name, email address and the details of what you are asking about.
  • Information about your use of the site — such as the listings and searches you save and the pages you visit, so that the site can offer those features and we can understand how it is used.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by our payment provider. Card and bank details are handled by them and do not pass through this site. We keep the transaction references and dates needed to know what has been paid for.
  • Technical information — standard information your browser sends, such as IP address, device and browser type.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies that are necessary for the site to work, such as keeping you signed in and protecting forms from misuse. We also use analytics, currently Google Analytics, to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. Analytics can be blocked in your browser without affecting the rest of the site.

How we use it

To provide and maintain the site and your account, to respond to what you have asked us, to provide services you have paid for, to send messages you have asked to receive, and to understand and improve how the site is used. We do not sell your personal information.

Who it is shared with

We share information with the service providers we rely on to run the site — currently our hosting provider, our email provider, our payment provider (PayPal) and our analytics provider (Google) — and only as far as they need it to provide that service. We may also disclose information where we are required to by law. Where you have asked us to make an introduction about a specific property, we pass on what is needed to do that.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as it is needed for the purposes above, and for as long as we are required to keep it by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to provide a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop sending you messages. Depending on where you live you may have further rights, including the right to complain to a data protection authority. Contact hello@akiyainjapan.com and we will deal with your request.

Children

The site is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
