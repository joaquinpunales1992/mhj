Privacy policy

What we collect, why we have it, who else sees it, and how to get rid of it.

Last updated {{ last_updated }}.

Who we are

My Akiya in Japan (akiyainjapan.com) lists houses for sale in Japan and sells a small number of research and advice services around them. {% if legal_entity %}It is operated by {{ legal_entity }}.{% endif %} You can reach a human at hello@akiyainjapan.com, which is also where privacy requests go.

What we collect

If you make an account

Your email address, and either a password (which we store only as a hash — we cannot read it) or the fact that you signed in with Google. If you use Google sign-in, Google tells us your email address and name; it does not give us access to anything else in your Google account.

If you send us something

The site has several forms, and each stores what you type into it:

  • Enquiring about a house — your name, email, message, the listing you were looking at, and, when you fill them in, the regions you are interested in, your budget, your timeline, and whether you have been to Japan.
  • Booking a consultation — your name, email, time zone, any notes you add, and the slot you booked.
  • Requesting an inspection or a desk report — your name, email, the listing, and your notes about it.
  • Asking for access to a premium listing — your email and the listing.

We also keep our own notes against these requests, so that whoever picks one up knows what has already been said.

What the site records as you use it

  • Properties you save and searches you save (the city and price range, and whether you asked to be notified).
  • Which listings you have opened. Signed-in visitors get a set number of listing views before Pro is needed, and the count has to follow your account rather than your browser, so we store one row per property you have opened.

If you pay us

Payments are handled by PayPal. We never see your card or bank details — they do not pass through this site. What we store is what PayPal tells us afterwards: the subscription, order and capture identifiers, the amount, the currency, and the dates. We use those to know whether your access is current.

Cookies and analytics

The site sets a session cookie and a CSRF cookie when you sign in or submit a form. Those are what keep you logged in and stop a third-party site submitting forms as you; the site cannot work without them.

We also use Google Analytics (property G-QBY9Y9CMPS) to see how many people arrive, from where, and which pages they read. It sets its own cookies and tells Google your IP address and the pages you visited. It is loaded after the rest of the page so it does not slow the listings down.

If you would rather not be measured, browser-level blocking of Google Analytics is effective here and breaks nothing on the site.

Why we have it

To run your account and keep you signed in; to answer what you asked us; to provide and gate the things you have paid for; to send the newsletter to subscribers; and to understand how much traffic the site gets and what people read. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Who else sees it

  • PayPal — payments and subscriptions.
  • Google — Analytics, and sign-in if you use it.
  • Our email provider — the mail we send you passes through it.
  • Our hosting provider — the server the site runs on.

Nobody else. Enquiries about a specific house may be forwarded to the agent or seller handling that listing, but only when you have asked us to make an introduction, and we will tell you when we do.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Data is stored on our hosting provider's servers. We keep account data for as long as the account exists. Enquiries and requests are kept while we are dealing with them and for a reasonable period afterwards, because a buyer who comes back a year later expects us to remember the conversation. Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require.

What you can ask for

Email hello@akiyainjapan.com and you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your account and the data attached to it;
  • send you a copy of what you gave us;
  • stop sending you email.

We will not make you explain why. Depending on where you live you may also have a right to complain to a data protection regulator, and nothing here takes that away.

Children

The site is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it.
