{% comment %} Loading placeholder for photographs, site-wide. Property photos are served from the source listing sites (homes.jp, SUUMO) and can be slow, so on a poor connection the grids and galleries render as a scatter of empty boxes. This gives every photo a shimmering placeholder that its own pixels cover once decoded. Two deliberate choices: - The shimmer is painted as the 's own background, so no wrapper markup is needed and there is no layout shift. A loading has no content, so its background shows; once the image paints it sits on top. - Applied by absence of a class rather than presence, so images injected by JavaScript (the map's card list and marker popups) are covered too without each call site remembering to opt in. Excluded: anything under STATIC_URL. Those are local UI assets — social icons, placeholder art — which load instantly and are often small or transparent, where a shimmer would look like a bug. {% endcomment %}
